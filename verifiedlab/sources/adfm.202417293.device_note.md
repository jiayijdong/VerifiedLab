## Device schematic (listed top-down, as drawn)

- Au electrode, 80 nm, thermally evaporated (0.2–0.5 Å s-1)
- Spiro-OMeTAD hole-transport layer, spin-coated from a 72.3 mg/mL chlorobenzene solution with Li-TFSI and tBP
- Cr-MOF surface-treatment interlayer, spin-coated onto the formed absorber from 4 mg/mL in chlorobenzene (3000 rpm, 30 s), annealed 100 °C for 5 min
- FAPbI3 absorber, bandgap 1.55 eV; precursor PbI2 1.53 M / FAI 1.4 M with 0.5 M MACl in DMF/DMSO 8:1, antisolvent ethyl acetate, annealed 100 °C for 60 min
- SnO2 electron-transport layer, chemical bath deposition, annealed 170 °C for 60 min
- FTO glass

The device is a regular (n-i-p) architecture.

## Photovoltaic performance (reverse scan, AM1.5G, 1 sun)

Voc 1.164 V, Jsc 25.29 mA/cm2, FF 84.3 %, PCE 24.81 %. Stabilized output 24.3 %. The scan
protocol is not described in this note.

## Stability

Unencapsulated device stored in air at room temperature (~25 °C), relative humidity below 10 %:
89.17 % of the initial PCE retained after 2500 h.
